MODULE 1

Finance & Business Fundamentals

How money works — budgeting, saving, investing, and reading a financial statement.

Students build a solid base in personal and business finance. This module breaks down real-world financial concepts into clear, actionable lessons so students immediately understand how money moves in everyday life and inside a business.

Personal Finance & Budgeting
Accounting Basics
Financial Statements (P&L, Balance Sheet)
Time Value of Money
Business Terminology
Introduction to Investing

MODULE 2

Business Analysis

How to read a market, size an opportunity, and understand what makes a business tick.

Students learn to evaluate real businesses from the outside in — identifying market size, competitive positioning, and which business models actually work. Concepts are demystified with plain language and real examples.

Business Model Canvas
Market Sizing (How big is the opportunity?)
Competitive Analysis
Unit Economics (How does a company make money per customer?)
Financial Statement Analysis
Platform & Marketplace Models

MODULE 3

Business Strategy

Why some companies win and others fail — and the frameworks top CEOs use to plan growth.

Students learn how successful companies gain competitive advantage. This module introduces strategy frameworks used by real businesses to spot opportunities, outmaneuver competitors, and plan long-term growth — made accessible through real-world case studies.

Blue Ocean Strategy (Creating new markets)
Porter's Five Forces (Competitive analysis)
Ansoff Growth Matrix (How to grow a business)
Pricing Strategies
Corporate Growth Models
Rapid Scaling Strategies

Students move from thinking like a student to thinking like a founder. They learn how real startups are born, tested, and scaled — using the same frameworks taught at top business schools, simplified into action steps they can apply right now.

Lean Startup Methodology
Finding Product-Market Fit
Business Model Validation
Leading Yourself & Teams
AGILE & Project Management
Funding Readiness

Building on Module 1, students go deeper into business finance — learning how companies manage cash, get valued, and fund their growth. These skills translate directly into smarter financial decisions whether they run a business or invest in one.

Financial Statements Deep Dive

Types of Investors (Angels, VCs, Crowdfunding)
