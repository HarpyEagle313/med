General

YoungMBA is an online academy that provides MBA-style entrepreneurship education for young people ages 9-19. We teach real-world business skills including financial literacy, marketing, AI tools, leadership, and startup execution through hands-on projects and expert instruction.
Our programs are designed for ambitious young people between ages 9-19 who are interested in business, entrepreneurship, technology, and leadership. Whether your child dreams of starting a business or simply wants practical skills for the future, YoungMBA has a program for them.

Curriculum & Learning

Our comprehensive curriculum covers 10 core modules: Finance & Business Fundamentals, Business Analysis, Business Strategy, Entrepreneurship, Marketing, Accounting & Finance, Fundraising, Management, Digital Marketing, and AI in Business. Explore the full curriculum →
We use a Learn-Do-Reflect methodology. Students learn concepts through engaging content, apply them through real-world projects, and reflect on outcomes with mentor feedback. The curriculum progresses in complexity as students advance.
Yes! Upon completing any program, students receive a certificate of completion. This can be included in their portfolio for college applications or future career pursuits.
The Financial Foundations program is a 2-month intensive. The Live Masterclass includes weekly 90-minute sessions. The Self-Paced course allows students to learn at their own speed, typically taking 12-18 months for the full curriculum.
